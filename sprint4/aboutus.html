ABOUT US

IDENTITY

STLYIX is a digital fashion interface designed to connect users with personal designers and curated wardrobes. Operating at the intersection of fashion and technology, we provide a platform where individuality meets digital innovation.

Our system enables users to discover designers, build curated wardrobes, and receive personalized styling experiences. Designers can showcase their creativity, gain exposure, and connect with clients who value their craft.

DUAL DASHBOARD

USER INTERFACE

Wardrobe curation, designer booking, style management, and personalized recommendations.

DESIGNER INTERFACE

Portfolio showcasing, client interaction, brand growth analytics, and creative tools.

CORE OBJECTIVES

We are building a space where fashion meets technology — where creativity becomes accessible, collaborative, and community-driven. Our mission is to democratize fashion by connecting creators with enthusiasts through a seamless digital experience.

Through innovative features like the digital closet, like system, and personalized shopping experience, STLYIX aims to revolutionize how people discover and interact with fashion.
